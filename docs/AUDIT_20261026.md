## Checked, no defect found (asserted by tests in `tests/test_audit_r24.py`)
* Incomplete research cannot finalize: every mandatory check that is not VERIFIED/N/A blocks the tier across 5 category/stage combos; Low snapshot confidence blocks; no X data blocks.
* Stale X data (>72h) caps hype confidence Low → unranked. Research uses the job's own snapshot or one within `X_TTL_H`.
* Insufficient samples: <3 posts → Low/unranked; <10 mentions → community/organic FAILED.
* Viral single post (GigaLegends 1 post, Nuloraio 87% in one post) cannot reach High confidence.
* Duplicate text / repeat authors / one-thread concentration reduce Community and add risk flags; giveaway, paid-promo and high promo ratio lower genuineness.
* Hype alone cannot force Tier 1 (quality cap). Artwork is never VERIFIED from hashes only. Missing team info is "not negative".
* Conflicting sourced claims are listed and cap confidence at Medium.
* Repeated scoring on identical inputs is deterministic.
* Sorsa / smart-account signals remain advisory (`affects_tier: False`, existing tests).

## Benchmark / accuracy
Weights and tier thresholds are **unchanged**. All `regression_expectations.json` cases and the 3 `scan3` real
cases produce the same outcomes before and after. On the real data (scan3 fixtures + all 177 evidence rows in the
production DB) the new de-dup and mismatch rules change nothing (0 duplicates, 0 mismatch links), so **no accuracy
improvement is claimed**.

## Regression ground truth gap
`manual_reviews` in the DB is empty — there are no user-confirmed "correct" tiers. `tests/fixtures/legacy_tier_corrections.json`
records 5 real v1-legacy snapshots (DB ids) whose tier/confidence violate the documented v2 rules (e.g. Tier 2 / High from
1–2 posts; Minehood Tier 3 and Tier 1 for identical metrics) and asserts the current v2 outcome. These expectations are the
documented algorithm's outputs, not independent ground truth.

## Remaining limitations
* De-dup is exact-match only; reworded duplicate claims from the same URL still count.
* Live Sorsa/X follow checks not exercised (no `X_BEARER_TOKEN` in `.env`).
* `organic` check is VERIFIED whenever the sample is big enough, even with a high promo ratio (the ratio lowers the
  genuineness factor instead) — by design per docs, not changed.
* Discord `/review` tier option accepts 1–9; the real range is validated by `add_review` against the configured tiers.
